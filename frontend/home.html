About the Platform

What is RemoteShiksha?

RemoteShiksha is a modern Learning Management System built for the next generation of learners and educators in India. Whether you're in a city or a village — if you have internet, you have a classroom.

🌍
Built for Bharat
Designed keeping rural and urban students in mind — lightweight, fast, and works on low bandwidth.
🔒
Secure & Role-Based
Students, teachers and admins each have their own tailored experience with secure JWT authentication.
🤖
AI-Powered Learning
Ask your AI Teacher anything, anytime — instant explanations, examples and step-by-step guidance, 24/7.
🎓
Your Classroom, Reimagined
No building. No commute. Just pure learning — anywhere, anytime, on any device.

How It Works

Up and running in 3 steps

Getting started on RemoteShiksha is simple — whether you're here to learn or teach.

1
Create your account
Register in seconds. Choose your role — student, teacher, or get assigned as admin.
2
Join or create a course
Students browse and enroll in courses. Teachers create courses and add lectures instantly.
3
Learn & grow
Watch lectures, attend live classes, chat in real-time, ask doubts, and get AI-powered help 24/7.
